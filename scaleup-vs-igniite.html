SCALEUP vs IGNIITE: scale vs. start

Updated July 29, 2026 · Free educational guide · verify details at the official sources below

ARPA-E SCALEUP gives $5M–$20M to prior ARPA-E awardees to bridge toward commercial scale — you must own an invention from a previous ARPA-E award. IGNIITE gives up to ~$500K to individual early-career innovators to pursue a bold energy concept. One is for scaling a proven technology; the other is for launching a new one.

Two programs, opposite ends

ARPA-E runs several programs beyond its core focused and OPEN calls. Two of them get confused constantly because both have catchy names — but they sit at opposite ends of the funding lifecycle. SCALEUP is for the finish line; IGNIITE is for the starting gun.

SCALEUP: for graduates of ARPA-E

SCALEUP — Seeding Critical Advances for Leading Energy technologies with Untapped Potential — exists to bridge the gap between an ARPA-E-funded prototype and commercial scale. Awards run $5M–$20M; the 2025 round funded ten awardees with more than $70M. The catch: you must be a company that owns or controls a subject invention from a prior ARPA-E award, with a patent filed or issued. Small businesses are the prime; universities and DOE labs can't be. Antora Energy's ~$14.5M award is a good example of the scale involved.

IGNIITE: for people, not companies

IGNIITE — Inspiring Generations of New Innovators to Impact Technologies in Energy — funds individual early-career innovators: students, postdocs, and professionals early in their careers. Awards are up to about $500K, and the program wraps mentorship and networking around the money. Like lab fellowships, it's a way in for someone who doesn't yet have a company or a track record of federal awards.

If your project needs simulation

Scale-up milestones lean heavily on reliability and safety simulation; early IGNIITE work leans on fast concept modeling — we can arrange the right Ansys eval for either.

Which door is yours

If you already hold an ARPA-E award and need capital to scale, SCALEUP. If you're early-career with a bold idea and no ARPA-E history, IGNIITE — or a LEEP fellowship. If you're somewhere in between with a transformative concept, the standard focused or OPEN route is the main event.

Official sources: ARPA-E SCALEUP · ARPA-E IGNIITE 2025. Figures change; confirm on the official page before relying on them.
