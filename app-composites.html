Composites & damage tolerance: how Ansys helps

Composites buy you strength at a fraction of the weight — but failure is complex, layer-by-layer, and hard to test exhaustively. Simulation lets you design the layup and prove damage tolerance efficiently.

Illustrative simulation render. Actual studies are built to your geometry and requirements.

The Ansys tools that model it

  • Composites (ACP) — Full composite pre/post — ply layup definition, draping, and failure post-processing.
  • Mechanical — Progressive-damage and delamination analysis of laminates under real loads.
  • LS-DYNA — Impact, crush, and ballistic response of composite structures.
