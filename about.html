What this site is

A free, informational website. It aggregates public information about AFWERX / SpaceWERX — STRATFI & TACFI and related U.S. funding for startups, points you to each official source, and explains how engineering simulation (Ansys®) maps to the technology those funders back — including how early-stage teams can access simulation software through evaluation licenses and the Ansys Startup Program. It is operated in connection with an independent, authorized Ansys channel-partner sales organization.

About the information here

Amounts, deadlines, eligibility, and event dates are summarized from public sources and change constantly. They may be outdated or wrong by the time you read them. Always verify on the official source — every program and event links to one.

If you email us

We'll reply fast — usually under 20 minutes during business hours — with plain answers about simulation tooling and the access paths that may fit. That reply is information, not a commitment. No cost, no obligation, and you can stop at any point.
