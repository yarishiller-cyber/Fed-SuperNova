About this site

Purely informational. Nothing here is guaranteed, promised, or owed.

Updated July 28, 2026

What this site is

This is a free, informational website. It does two things:

  • It aggregates public information about U.S. funding sources for startups and small businesses — SBIR/STTR and other federal programs, private capital, and the industry events where founders meet funders — and points you to each official source.
  • It explains engineering simulation: what it's used for, which Ansys® tools map to which engineering problems, and the paths that exist for early-stage teams to access simulation software, including evaluation licenses and the Ansys Startup Program.

The site is operated in connection with an independent, authorized Ansys channel-partner sales organization. If you contact us, that's who you'll be talking to: people whose day job is getting simulation software and engineering support into companies' hands.

About the information here

Program amounts, deadlines, eligibility rules, and event dates are summarized from public sources for convenience and change constantly. They may be outdated or wrong by the time you read them. Always verify everything on the official source — every program and event on this site links to one. Key official starting points:

  • SBIR.gov — the official home of the SBIR/STTR programs
  • Ansys Startup Program and Ansys for Students — official Ansys pages
  • Each funding program's own site, linked from the Funding Finder

If you email us

We'll reply fast — usually in under 20 minutes during business hours — with plain answers about simulation tooling and the access paths that may fit your situation. That reply is information, not a commitment. There is no cost and no obligation, and you can stop talking to us at any point.

The one thing we'd tell every founder

If your product's physics has to be proven — airflow, stress, heat, signal — you will eventually need simulation. Paths exist to start at no cost while you're still pre-funding. Ask us, ask Ansys, or ask any channel partner; just don't wait until a proposal deadline to find out.
