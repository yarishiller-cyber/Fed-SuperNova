Funding Events · 2026–2027

Where founders go to find the money.

The other half of hunting for funding is being in the room. This is a running calendar of the U.S. events where startups and student founders meet the people who hand out non-dilutive money: SBIR program managers, AFWERX and SpaceWERX teams, DARPA, defense primes, and the investors who back dual-use companies. Dates and details below, with a link straight to each official page.

Dates shift and some 2027 dates aren't published yet. Where a date isn't locked we say so; always confirm on the official site before you book travel.

Defense

Defense innovation & industry

Where DoD program managers, primes and defense VCs gather. The fastest rooms to meet the people who write non-dilutive checks.

Aug 25–27, 2026

Fed Supernova 2026

Capital Factory · Austin, TX

The reason this site is named what it is. AFWERX, SpaceWERX and DIU program managers hold office hours, startups pitch, and defense VCs are everywhere. If you go to one defense event, go to this.

Official site ↗ fedsupernova.com
Sep 8–10, 2026

NDIA Emerging Technologies for Defense

National Defense Industrial Association · Washington, DC

Program offices brief real business opportunities and a non-traditional vendor can get in front of DoD tech leadership without a prime in the way.

Official site ↗ ndiatechexpo.org
Sep 14–16, 2026

AFA Air, Space & Cyber Conference

Air & Space Forces Association · National Harbor, MD

~300 exhibits and direct access to Air Force and Space Force acquisition executives. Strong for airpower, space, autonomy and AI startups.

Official site ↗ afa.org
Oct 12–14, 2026

AUSA Annual Meeting & Exposition

Association of the U.S. Army · Washington, DC

The biggest land-power show in the country. The Innovators Corner pitch stage and the xTech prize ecosystem make it a real funding on-ramp, not just a trade show.

Official site ↗ meetings.ausa.org
Dec 4–5, 2026

Reagan National Defense Forum

Reagan Foundation & Institute · Simi Valley, CA

Less a pitch venue, more the room where Pentagon leadership, defense-tech capital and policy all show up at once. Go to build relationships above your weight class.

Official site ↗ reaganfoundation.org
Apr 4–7, 2027

Navy League Sea-Air-Space

Navy League of the United States · National Harbor, MD

The largest U.S. maritime expo. If you build anything for the Navy, Marines or Coast Guard, this is where the program managers are.

Official site ↗ seaairspace.org
April 2027 (confirm dates)

Space Symposium (42nd)

Space Foundation · Colorado Springs, CO

The premier national-security-space gathering. SpaceWERX and USSF access, startup programming, and investors under one roof at The Broadmoor.

Official site ↗ spacesymposium.org
Typically August (2027 TBD)

Small Satellite Conference (SmallSat)

Utah State University / AIAA · Logan / Salt Lake City, UT

The densest concentration of space primes, government buyers and investors of any space event. Space-hardware teams find partners and SBIR leads here.

Official site ↗ smallsat.org
Recurring, 2026–2027

AFWERX / SpaceWERX Pitch Days

AFWERX / SpaceWERX (AFRL) · Austin, National Harbor & others

This isn't a conference, it's the funding event. Same-day SBIR/STTR pitch-to-award decisions and direct program-manager access. Watch the calendar and apply.

Official site ↗ afwerx.com
Rolling — check the calendar

DARPA Proposers Days & DARPA Forward

DARPA · Arlington, VA & host campuses

The only way to talk to a DARPA program manager before you write a proposal. Proposers Days run continuously against active programs.

Official site ↗ darpa.mil
Government

Government SBIR / STTR

Run by the agencies themselves. Meet program directors, learn how proposals are scored, and map the non-dilutive money across all 11 SBIR agencies.

Typically May (virtual)

America's Seed Fund Week (National SBIR Week)

U.S. Small Business Administration · Virtual / national

Free, virtual, and connects you to all 11 federal SBIR/STTR agencies in a week. The 'Meet the Program Director' sessions alone are worth it.

Official site ↗ americasseedfund.us
Sep 22–24, 2026

Defense TechConnect Innovation Summit (Fall)

TechConnect · National Harbor, MD

250+ DoD scouts, 50+ contracting offices, and roughly $4B of SBIR/STTR seed funding represented in one place, plus a Defense Innovation Challenge pitch competition.

Official site ↗ events.techconnect.org
Sep 22–24, 2026

America's Seed Fund Summit (Fall Innovation Conference)

SBA — America's Seed Fund · Washington, DC area

The in-person counterpart to SBIR Week. Book one-on-ones with federal SBIR/STTR program managers and walk the agency innovation showcases.

Official site ↗ americasseedfund.us
Mar 23–25, 2027

SBIR/STTR Spring Innovation Conference

TechConnect · Boston, MA

Program-manager one-on-ones across agencies and proposal workshops for the government's largest early-stage R&D programs. Boston in the spring.

Official site ↗ techconnect.org
Regional stops, summer–fall

SBIR Road Tour

SBA + federal agencies · Multiple U.S. regions

Agency program managers come to your region. Real face-time, non-dilutive funding education, and local ecosystem connections without flying to DC.

Official site ↗ americasseedfund.us
Rolling webinars & office hours

NSF SBIR / Project Pitch (rolling)

National Science Foundation · Virtual / national

NSF takes no equity and funds deep tech up to ~$2M. The Project Pitch gives you direct program-director feedback before you commit to a full proposal.

Official site ↗ seedfund.nsf.gov
Private capital

Startup & venture capital

Where private capital, accelerators and press concentrate. Useful when you're raising a round alongside your non-dilutive strategy.

Jan 6–9, 2027

CES 2027

Consumer Technology Association · Las Vegas, NV

The world's largest tech show now has real defense, space and AI presence. Eureka Park is the startup zone, and corporate VCs walk it looking for deals.

Official site ↗ ces.tech
Typically March (2027 TBD)

a16z American Dynamism Summit

Andreessen Horowitz · Washington, DC

The signaling venue for founders building in the national interest. Concentrated access to the leading dual-use VC and its policymaker network.

Official site ↗ a16z.com
Mar 15–21, 2027

SXSW 2027

South by Southwest · Austin, TX

The SXSW Pitch competition, investor meetups, and Austin's Capital Factory ecosystem next door. Good for dual-use teams with a consumer angle.

Official site ↗ sxsw.com
Oct 13–15, 2026

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026

TechCrunch · San Francisco, CA

Startup Battlefield is a genuine funding catalyst, and the VC density in the room is hard to match. Press visibility is a bonus.

Official site ↗ techcrunch.com
Fall batch: Dec 2, 2026

Y Combinator Demo Day

Y Combinator · San Francisco, CA

If you're in a batch, this is the highest-leverage investor day in the country and rounds often close off it. If you're not, it's a reminder of what to apply to.

Official site ↗ ycombinator.com
Students

University & student competitions

Pitch competitions with real prize money and investor judges. Some of the best early, non-dilutive capital a student or pre-incorporation team can win.

Typically April (2027 TBD)

Rice Business Plan Competition

Rice Alliance / Rice University · Houston, TX

The world's richest graduate startup competition: $1M+ in cash and investment, ~300 investor judges, and a strong deep-tech field. For many student teams it is the funding round.

Official site ↗ rbpc.rice.edu
Pitch Dec, Launch ~May

MIT $100K Entrepreneurship Competition

MIT · Cambridge, MA

A year-long, three-stage competition with a $100K grand prize and heavy hard-tech judging. Great structure for a student team turning research into a company.

Official site ↗ mit100k.org
Typically spring

Hacking for Defense (H4D) National Finals

BMNT / Common Mission Project · Stanford + campuses nationwide

A university course that puts student teams on real DoD problem sets, with sponsor relationships and a proven pipeline into defense-tech startups and follow-on SBIR/DIU funding.

Official site ↗ h4d.us
Apr 16–17, 2027

Baylor New Venture Competition

Baylor University · Waco, TX

$200K+ in cash prizes, industry mentorship, and investor judges, open to student teams and recent alumni worldwide.

Official site ↗ hankamer.baylor.edu
Spring finals (2027 TBD)

CU Boulder New Venture Challenge — Deep Tech

Venture Partners at CU Boulder · Boulder, CO

A collegiate competition built specifically for hard-science ventures, with up to $50K in prizes and deep-tech mentorship. On-point for dual-use founders.

Official site ↗ colorado.edu
