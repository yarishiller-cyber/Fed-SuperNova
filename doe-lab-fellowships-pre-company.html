DOE lab fellowships for pre-company founders

Updated July 29, 2026 · Free educational guide · verify details at the official sources below

The DOE Lab-Embedded Entrepreneurship Program (LEEP) — Activate/Cyclotron Road, Innovation Crossroads, Chain Reaction, and West Gate — funds individuals, not companies. Fellows get roughly a $100K living stipend plus ~$100K–$175K in R&D and relocation over two years, with national-lab access. You apply as a person, often before incorporating, and no equity is taken.

Money before you're a company

Most federal funding assumes you already have an entity, a DUNS/UEI, and a track record. LEEP doesn't. The Lab-Embedded Entrepreneurship Program funds individuals with a hard-tech energy concept — you can apply before you've incorporated, and many fellows form their company during the fellowship. It's the friendliest on-ramp in the whole ecosystem for a scientist with an idea and no company yet.

The four nodes

There are four nodes, each hosted at a national lab: Activate / Cyclotron Road at Lawrence Berkeley (the original, run with the nonprofit Activate), Innovation Crossroads at Oak Ridge, Chain Reaction Innovations at Argonne, and West Gate at NREL in Golden, Colorado. Each embeds you in a lab with world-class equipment and staff scientists.

What you get

The package runs two years and generally includes a living stipend around $100K, on the order of $100K–$175K in R&D funding, healthcare, a travel allowance, relocation support, and — critically — access to national-lab facilities you could never afford to build. Totals typically exceed $300K over the two years. It's non-dilutive; no one takes equity in the company you go on to form.

If your project needs simulation

A lab fellowship gives you national-lab facilities; pairing that with an Ansys eval lets you iterate designs in software between physical experiments — we can help set that up.

Who gets in

These are competitive and selective. Reviewers look for technical depth, a concept with real energy impact, and a person who can plausibly become a founder — not just a great researcher. Fervo Energy, Sublime Systems, and many others trace back to these fellowships. See who gets funded for the pattern.

How to apply

Each node runs its own annual cohort call, but Activate's national fellowship application typically closes in late October for the cohort that starts the following year. Apply through activate.org/apply or the individual node sites. Start early — a strong application needs a crisp technical thesis, not just enthusiasm.

Official sources: DOE LEEP program · Activate fellowship. Figures change; confirm on the official page before relying on them.
