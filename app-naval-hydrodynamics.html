Naval hydrodynamics & signature: how Ansys helps

Under the waterline, drag, cavitation, and acoustic signature decide range and detectability. Hydrodynamic simulation lets maritime teams refine hulls and propulsors without endless tow-tank runs.

Illustrative simulation render. Actual studies are built to your geometry and requirements.

The Ansys tools that model it

  • Fluent — Free-surface and multiphase CFD for hull resistance, propulsor performance, and cavitation.
  • Aqwa — Hydrodynamic diffraction and seakeeping of floating platforms in real sea states.
  • Mechanical — Structural response, slamming loads, and vibration of hulls and appendages.
