The Ansys Startup Program

Built for early-stage companies — and most defense founders qualify

The program gives young companies bundled, discounted access to the same simulation firepower the primes use. Eligibility is generous, which is why it's the lowest-friction way to get Ansys into your hands.

Eligibility at a glance

  • Privately held company
  • Less than 10 years old
  • Under $5M in annual revenue
  • Not an engineering-consulting / simulation-services firm

Not sure if you qualify? That's exactly the kind of question a quick call answers. Eligibility and pricing are confirmed case-by-case.
