Before you email

Questions founders actually ask us

Is Ansys really free for my startup?
You can start for free. We set up free Ansys evaluation licenses for the exact tools your project needs, plus free time with an application engineer to apply them to your MVP. For longer-term access, the Ansys Startup Program gives eligible early-stage companies deeply discounted licences. Email us and we'll work out which path fits.
Who qualifies for the Ansys Startup Program?
Privately held companies under 10 years old with less than $5M in annual revenue that aren't engineering-consulting or simulation-services firms. Most defense and dual-use startups qualify. We confirm your eligibility for free, no strings attached.
Can I get Ansys before I've incorporated?
Yes. Students can download free Ansys Student software today, and if you're a pre-incorporation team we can still arrange a proper evaluation. Tell us what you're building and we'll sort it out.
What does the free engineering support cover?
Time with an application engineer to help you pick the right physics, build the mesh, run the studies and read the results, so your simulation holds up when a reviewer or investor pushes on it. We stick to software and engineering. We never touch your grant paperwork.
Where we stop, on purpose

We supply simulation, not application advice.

We don't prepare, review, or advise on grant, SBIR/STTR, or contract applications, and we don't fill out any documents for you. Our lane is simulation software and the engineering to build and validate your technology. Staying in that lane keeps us clear of regulated advisory territory, and keeps us sharp at the one thing we actually do.
