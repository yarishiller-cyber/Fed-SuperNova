Every AFWERX / SpaceWERX — STRATFI & TACFI door, in one place.

Contract

AFWERX Open Topic SBIR/STTR Phase I

The entry door for dual-use tech that the Air Force didn't ask for by name. A short feasibility study to prove your idea is real and has a defense use case.

  • ~$75K (SBIR) / ~$110K (STTR) · Non-dilutive
  • US small business <500 employees, majority US-owned and controlled
  • periodic solicitation windows via DSIP

How to apply: Register in SAM.gov and the DoD SBIR/STTR portal (DSIP), then submit a short pitch/proposal against an open AFWERX topic. Phase I feasibility runs roughly 3 months.

Contract

AFWERX SBIR/STTR Phase II

Prototype and demonstrate the technology with a real Air or Space Force end user. This is where the money and the customer relationship get serious.

  • ~$1.25M–$1.9M · Non-dilutive
  • US small business with a qualifying Phase I (or a Direct-to-Phase-II customer memorandum)
  • periodic solicitation windows via DSIP

How to apply: Follow a Phase I award, or enter via Direct-to-Phase-II with a signed Customer Memorandum and a prototype-ready solution. Submitted through DSIP.

Official source ↗

TACFI — Tactical Funding Increase

A bridge for a strong Phase II that needs more runway. TACFI adds SBIR dollars on top of your existing work as long as you bring matching money.

  • ~$375K–$2M over ~2 years · Non-dilutive
  • Active or recent Phase II awardee with a committed 1:1 match
  • periodic Notices of Opportunity

How to apply: Secure at least $1 of other (non-SBIR/STTR) government or private funding for every $1 of TACFI SBIR funds, then apply against an open Notice of Opportunity.

Official source ↗

Matching

STRATFI — Strategic Funding Increase

The big transition tool that pushes a proven Phase II toward a program of record. The match is bigger, and so is the check.

  • ~$3M–$15M over ~4 years · Non-dilutive
  • Phase II awardee with committed matching funds and a government customer
  • periodic Notices of Opportunity

How to apply: Line up matching money — every $1 of SBIR is matched by $2 of other government funds, OR $1 government + $2 private — then apply through the STRATFI/TACFI Notice of Opportunity.

Official source ↗

Strategic Breakthrough Award (new $30M Phase II tier)

Created by the April 2026 SBIR/STTR reauthorization. A sequential Phase II vehicle with a $30M ceiling built to carry deep-tech across the 'valley of death' to Phase III.

  • up to ~$30M · Non-dilutive
  • Small business holding at least one prior SBIR or STTR Phase II award
  • rolling as agencies stand up the mechanism (authorized through FY2031)

How to apply: Details are still being implemented by the Department of the Air Force post-reauthorization; watch AFWERX and DSIP for the first Strategic Breakthrough solicitations.

Official source ↗

Direct to Phase II (D2P2)

Skips Phase I for teams that already have mature technology and an Air Force customer. You still prove feasibility — you just do it with your own prior work.

  • ~$1.25M · Non-dilutive
  • Small business with prototype-ready tech and a documented government customer
  • periodic solicitation windows via DSIP

How to apply: Submit a Phase II proposal with a signed Customer Memorandum demonstrating an end user, in place of a Phase I award.

SpaceWERX Open Topic SBIR/STTR

The Space Force twin of AFWERX Open Topic. Same ladder, aimed at on-orbit, launch, and space-domain-awareness technology.

  • ~$75K (Phase I) / ~$1.25M–$1.9M (Phase II) · Non-dilutive
  • US small business <500 employees, majority US-owned
  • periodic solicitation windows via DSIP

How to apply: Register in SAM.gov and DSIP, then submit against an open SpaceWERX topic. TACFI/STRATFI transition applies on the Space side too.

SpaceWERX Orbital Prime

A challenge-style program seeding the In-space Servicing, Assembly and Manufacturing (ISAM) market, using active debris remediation as the anchor use case.

  • ~$250K (Phase I) / ~$1.7M (Direct-to-Phase-II) · Non-dilutive
  • US small business (STTR variants pair with a research institution)
  • periodic topic releases

How to apply: Apply against an open Orbital Prime topic in DSIP; both SBIR and STTR paths feed toward Strategic Financing at Phase III.

Contract

AFWERX Challenges / Focused Open Topics

For 2026 the Air Force is steering Open Topic toward specific mission gaps ('focused open topics'), with directed topics now outweighing pure open ones.

  • varies (typically Phase I–II SBIR scale) · Non-dilutive
  • US small business meeting SBIR/STTR rules
  • topic-driven, announced on afwerx.com and DSIP

How to apply: Read the topic's mission gap carefully and align your pitch to the named need — focused topics reward a tight problem-solution fit over a generic dual-use story.

AFVentures / Strategic Financing pathway

AFVentures is the AFWERX arm that runs the investment-and-transition side — the umbrella over TACFI, STRATFI, and private-capital matching that turns SBIR wins into scale.

  • leverages SBIR + matched government/private capital · Non-dilutive (SBIR portion)
  • Phase II awardees pursuing transition to a program of record
  • aligned to STRATFI/TACFI Notices of Opportunity

How to apply: Build a government customer and private co-investment early; AFVentures evaluates transition potential and matching commitments, not just the technology.

The lifecycle

How funding flows — and where simulation fits

01

Phase I (Feasibility)

~$75K · ~3 months

Prove the concept is technically real and has a defense use case.

A single well-chosen simulation — a CFD run, a modal analysis, an antenna pattern — is often the cheapest way to show feasibility on paper before any hardware exists.

02

Phase II (Prototype)

~$1.25M–$1.9M · ~15–24 months

Build and demonstrate a prototype with a real Air/Space Force end user.

Digital-twin and full-physics models (structural, thermal, aero, RF) cut test iterations and let you show margins the government reviewer wants to see.

03

TACFI (Tactical bridge)

~$375K–$2M, 1:1 match · ~2 years

Extend a strong Phase II and close a specific capability gap.

Simulation makes the matching money go further — you validate design changes virtually instead of burning bridge funds on rebuilds.

04

STRATFI (Strategic transition)

~$3M–$15M, matched · ~4 years

Scale a proven prototype toward a program of record.

At this stage simulation supports qualification and requirements verification — hypersonic aerothermal, vibration, thermal-vacuum — the evidence a program office needs.

05

Strategic Breakthrough (new)

up to ~$30M · sequential Phase II

Carry breakthrough tech across the valley of death to Phase III fielding.

High-fidelity, validated models become part of the deliverable — the certification-grade analysis that lets a capability transition without a decade of physical testing.
