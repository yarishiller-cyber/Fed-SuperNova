Funding Finder

Every ARPA-E & DOE Energy Innovation Funding door, in one place.

Real programs, real numbers, real official links — verified July 29, 2026. Amounts and deadlines change constantly, so confirm on each official source before you rely on it. Not sure which fits what you're building? Ask us.

Grant

ARPA-E OPEN Solicitation

ARPA-E's flagship, technology-agnostic call — any transformative energy idea, not tied to a focused program area. Runs roughly every three years.

  • $250K–$10M (~$3M typical; up to 36 months) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. entities — startups, small businesses, universities, national labs, and teams. A domestic prime applicant is required; teaming is encouraged.
  • ~every 3 years (last OPEN: 2024)

How to apply: Submit a mandatory 4-page concept paper in ARPA-E eXCHANGE; if encouraged, submit a full application. ~20% cost share requested.

Grant

ARPA-E Focused Programs

Targeted, director-driven programs on a specific technical challenge (e.g. fusion materials, long-duration storage, industrial heat). A handful launch each year.

  • $250K–$10M per award (program budgets ~$20M–$50M) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. teams whose approach fits the program's specific metrics and categories of interest.
  • ~4 new programs per year, rolling

How to apply: Watch ARPA-E eXCHANGE for each program's FOA; concept paper first, then full application if encouraged. ~5–20% cost share by applicant type.

Official source ↗

Grant

ARPA-E SCALEUP

Seeding Critical Advances for Leading Energy technologies with Untapped Potential — helps prior ARPA-E awardees bridge the gap to commercial scale.

  • $5M–$20M (2025 round: 10 awardees, $70M+) · Non-dilutive
  • Only companies that own/control a subject invention from a prior ARPA-E award; small businesses as prime. Higher-ed and DOE labs cannot be prime.
  • periodic (open + focused editions)

How to apply: Respond to the SCALEUP FOA in eXCHANGE with a concept paper; higher cost share applies (often ~30%+ at scale).

Official source ↗

Grant

ARPA-E IGNIITE

Inspiring Generations of New Innovators to Impact Technologies in Energy — funds individual early-career innovators to pursue a bold energy concept.

  • ≤$500K per project (2025 cohort: ~$10M total) · Non-dilutive
  • Early-career individuals — students, postdocs, and professionals early in their careers, including those not yet at an established company.
  • annual (installments launched 2024, 2025)

How to apply: Submit an individual application through eXCHANGE; a mentorship and networking component runs alongside the funding.

Official source ↗

Grant

ARPA-E SBIR / STTR

ARPA-E's small-business set-aside for high-risk energy R&D, run through its focused and open topics for eligible small businesses.

  • Phase I ~$250K; Phase II ~$1M+ (follow-on) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small businesses (STTR requires a research-institution partner). Majority U.S.-owned; size limits apply.
  • topic-dependent, aligned to FOAs

How to apply: Apply against the relevant SBIR/STTR topic in eXCHANGE; Phase I proves feasibility before a Phase II bid.

Official source ↗

Grant

DOE Lab-Embedded Entrepreneurship Program (LEEP)

Two-year fellowships that embed founders inside a national lab — Activate/Cyclotron Road (Berkeley), Innovation Crossroads (ORNL), Chain Reaction (Argonne), West Gate (NREL). The most pre-incorporation-friendly money in the ecosystem.

  • ~$100K living stipend + ~$100K–$175K R&D + relocation (over 2 yrs; >$300K total) · Non-dilutive
  • Individuals with a hard-tech energy concept — you apply as a person, often before forming a company. No equity taken.
  • annual cohorts (apply the year before)

How to apply: Apply to a specific node; Activate's national fellowship application typically closes in late October for the following cohort.

Official source ↗

Prize

American-Made Challenges (Prizes)

NREL/DOE cash-prize competitions run on HeroX — Solar Prize, Geothermal, heliostat, and more. No cost share, and startup-friendly milestone payouts plus lab-voucher support.

  • $50K per finalist up to ~$500K+; prize pools ~$4M per round · Non-dilutive
  • Anyone based in the U.S. with a marketable energy technology; individuals and teams welcome, no incorporation required to start.
  • rolling rounds by topic

How to apply: Register on HeroX for the active prize, submit for the Ready/Set/Go-style stages; finalists earn cash plus national-lab vouchers.

Official source ↗

Grant

DOE SBIR / STTR (Office of Science)

Department-wide small-business R&D grants across DOE's technology offices, administered by the Office of Science, with energy-relevant topics each cycle.

  • Phase I up to ~$250K (6–12 mo); Phase II larger · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small businesses (STTR needs a partnering research institution). A mandatory Letter of Intent precedes the full proposal.
  • annual, multiple releases (FY26 Phase I open)

How to apply: File the required LOI, then submit the full Phase I proposal against a chosen topic through the DOE SBIR/STTR portal.

Official source ↗

Loan

DOE Loan Programs (Energy Dominance Financing / Title 17)

Later-stage debt for capital-intensive energy projects, now run by the Office of Energy Dominance Financing. For first-of-a-kind commercial builds, not R&D.

  • Typically ≥$100M loan guarantees (no formal minimum) · Debt
  • Projects deploying qualifying energy technology in the U.S. that can absorb multimillion-dollar diligence costs — a scale-up tool, not a starting point.
  • rolling intake

How to apply: Engage LPO/OEDF early with a pre-application; expect extensive technical and financial due diligence.

The lifecycle

How funding flows — and where simulation fits

01

Concept Paper

$0 (gate to apply) · 4 pages, ~30-day window

Convince ARPA-E your idea is transformative, not incremental — the impact metric, the physics, why now.

A back-of-envelope simulation (a Fluent thermal sweep or a Mechanical stress estimate) lets you state a defensible performance target instead of a hopeful one.

02

Encourage / Discourage

$0 (feedback) · weeks after concept paper

ARPA-E signals whether to invest in a full application — an 'encourage' is a real filter, not a formality.

If encouraged, reviewers will probe feasibility; early simulation results give you concrete answers to the questions they'll ask.

03

Full Application

requested award · ~30–45 pages + budget

Detail the technical approach, milestones, team, and a credible path to the target metrics.

This is where simulation earns its keep — model the design space, show sensitivity to the risky parameters, and justify every milestone with physics.

04

Award & Performance

$250K–$10M (~$3M typical) · up to 36 months

Hit go/no-go milestones and prove the technology to a real-world benchmark.

Simulation shortens the build-test-break loop between milestones — iterate a design in software, then validate with fewer, better-chosen physical tests.

05

Scale-Up

$5M–$20M (SCALEUP); ≥$100M (loans) · multi-year

Bridge from a proven prototype toward commercial manufacturing and deployment.

At scale, simulation shifts to manufacturability, reliability, and safety cases — thermal runaway propagation, fatigue life, and system-level performance.
