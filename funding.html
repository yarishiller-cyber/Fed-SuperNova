Funding FAQ

The questions we get most about the money

What counts as non-dilutive funding?
Money you don't give up equity for: federal programs like SBIR/STTR, DARPA, AFWERX and SpaceWERX, DIU, plus grants, prizes and some loans. The Funding Finder above maps 27 sources, federal and private, and links straight to each official page.
Is SBIR/STTR still running in 2026?
Yes. The program lapsed for about six months (Oct 2025 to Mar 2026) and was reauthorized in March 2026 through FY2031, so agencies have reopened solicitations. Always confirm current deadlines and caps on each program's official page before you rely on a figure.
Do you help write my SBIR or grant application?
No. We provide simulation software and engineering only. We don't prepare, review or advise on any funding application, and we don't guarantee any outcome. What we do is make sure the technology behind your application is simulated and credible.
How do I actually get the money?
You apply directly with the agency or investor through the official links in the Funding Finder. Our part is the simulation: getting Ansys and an engineer into your hands so the physics behind your pitch is proven.

Found a program that fits your technology?

That's the moment simulation matters most. Talk to an engineer about the Ansys tools and support to build and validate what that program funds.
