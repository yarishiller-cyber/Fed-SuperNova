Every Army xTech & Army SBIR door, in one place.

Real programs, real numbers, real official links — verified July 29, 2026. Amounts and deadlines change constantly, so confirm on each official source before you rely on it. Not sure which fits what you're building? Ask us.

Prize

xTechSearch (open topic)

The Army's flagship open-topic prize competition — pitch any disruptive technology with an Army use case. The most recent round drew 900+ applicants and named 24 winners.

  • ~$30K per winner (multi-round) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S.-based small businesses; open topic, no pre-set problem statement
  • annual (numbered rounds)

How to apply: Submit a concept white paper, then pitch through elimination rounds; winners get an invitation to submit an Army SBIR Phase I proposal.

Prize

xTechPacific

Themed competition for technologies that strengthen long-distance operations across the Indo-Pacific — contested logistics, sustainment, comms and autonomy.

  • ~$345K pool (12 finalists x ~$20K; ~3 winners x ~$35K) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S.-based small businesses
  • annual

How to apply: White paper → finalist pitch → live experimentation event; winners may pursue Army SBIR Phase I (~$250K) or Direct-to-Phase-II (up to ~$1.9M).

Official source ↗

Prize

xTech|Inversion

Flips the usual model — the Army offers its own developed intellectual property and pays small businesses to commercialize it into fieldable products.

  • up to ~$1M pool (12 finalists x $20K; up to 5 winners x $150K) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small businesses willing to license/build on Army IP
  • annual

How to apply: White paper (submission window ~June each year) → finalist pitch to Army SMEs → winners may submit an Army SBIR Phase I proposal worth up to ~$300K.

Official source ↗

Prize

xTech|Kinetic Reach

Seeks force-sustainment solutions — autonomous logistics, recovery and resupply that keep a fighting force moving.

  • up to ~$1.5M pool (up to 15 finalists) · Non-dilutive
  • Eligible U.S. small businesses
  • one-time / periodic

How to apply: Part 1 concept evaluation selects finalists; Part 2 is a live Soldier exercise and pitch event (anticipated Nov 2026).

Prize

xTech|Edge Strike: Ground

Run by the FUZE xTech Program for novel autonomous ground vehicles and launched-effects solutions at the tactical edge.

  • up to ~$2M pool (15 finalists x $25K; up to 5 winners x $275K) · Non-dilutive
  • Eligible U.S. small businesses
  • one-time / periodic

How to apply: Concept submission → finalist selection → live experimentation finals; final winners receive the larger cash award.

Prize

xTech|Phantum

A fast, lower-dollar competition staged at the Southwest Mission Acceleration Center (SWMAC) in Arizona to surface emerging tech quickly.

  • up to ~$200K pool (16 finalists x $2.5K; up to 8 winners x $20K) · Non-dilutive
  • Eligible U.S. small businesses
  • one-time / periodic

How to apply: Submit a concept, pitch to Army/DoW subject-matter experts at the SWMAC facility; winners take the cash prize.

Prize

xTech|Live (pitch series)

A rolling series of on-site pitch events at commercial tech conferences — e.g. eMerge Americas — that let founders pitch the Army in person for prize funding.

  • up to ~$600K per event (varies) · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small businesses; register a short quad chart or pitch on-site
  • recurring at partner conferences

How to apply: Submit a brief quad chart before the deadline or register on-site to pitch during the conference.

Contract

Army Applied SBIR – Phase I

The feasibility phase of the Army's small-business R&D program, with invitation-style topics tied to real transition partners and often fed directly by xTech winners.

  • up to ~$250K · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small business (>50% U.S.-owned, <500 employees)
  • rolling topics via BAA

How to apply: Respond to an open topic in the Army SBIR Broad Agency Announcement; xTech winners get an exclusive lane to submit.

Official source ↗

Contract

Army SBIR – Phase II

The full R&D and prototyping phase, typically a 12–18 month effort to mature and demonstrate the technology proven in Phase I.

  • up to ~$2M · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small businesses with a completed/qualifying Phase I
  • by invitation after Phase I

How to apply: Submit a Phase II proposal against your Phase I work; award is competitive and tied to a transition path.

Official source ↗

Contract

Army SBIR – Direct-to-Phase-II (D2P2)

Skips Phase I for teams that can already show feasibility from other funding — a fast lane into a full prototyping contract, often used by xTech winners with mature tech.

  • up to ~$2M · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small business that performed Phase I-equivalent work with non-SBIR funds
  • rolling D2P2 topics

How to apply: Submit feasibility documentation plus a Phase II proposal against a D2P2 topic; no separate Phase I award required.

The lifecycle

How funding flows — and where simulation fits

01

Concept white paper

$0 (entry stage) · ~2-4 weeks to write

Proves the idea is real, novel and Army-relevant in a few pages — the first cut, often from 900+ applicants down to a shortlist.

A single early simulation figure — a stress contour, a CFD flow field, an antenna pattern — signals engineering rigor and separates a real concept from a slide deck.

02

Finalist pitch

~$2.5K–$25K cash per finalist · 1 day (live or virtual)

A short pitch to Army and DoW subject-matter experts; proves the team can defend the tech and the transition story under questioning.

Parametric studies answer the 'what if it's twice as heavy / hot / fast' questions SMEs ask on the spot, without waiting on a new physical build.

03

Live experimentation / winner selection

~$20K–$275K additional cash · days to two weeks

Winners often demonstrate at a Soldier-shaped experimentation event; proves the hardware survives contact with real users and real conditions.

Simulation predicts failure modes before the field event, so you show up with a design that already survived thermal, blast and vibration on screen.

04

Army SBIR Phase I

up to ~$250K · ~3-6 months

Feasibility contract on the exclusive lane xTech winners earn — proves the technical approach against a real transition partner's need.

Phase I is where a validated simulation model becomes the backbone of the technical volume and the risk-reduction story.

05

Phase II / Direct-to-Phase-II

up to ~$2M · ~12-18 months

Full prototyping and demonstration with a named transition path toward a program of record.

High-fidelity multiphysics (structural + thermal + EM) replaces expensive test-fail-rebuild cycles and accelerates the qualification campaign.
