Production

APFIT — Accelerate the Procurement and Fielding of Innovative Technologies

A DoD procurement bridge that BUYS production quantities of already-proven small-business and non-traditional technology to pull it across the valley of death and into the field.

  • avg >$30M; up to $50M (statutory cap; record ~$49.7M) · Non-dilutive
  • Small businesses & non-traditional defense contractors with a mature, demonstrated capability (roughly TRL 8+); the tech must have a DoD end user that wants it.
  • annual (nomination cycle per fiscal year)

How to apply: You cannot apply directly. A DoD program office / component must NOMINATE your project — so the real work is winning an internal sponsor who will put you forward. See our APFIT explainer.

Loan

DoD Office of Strategic Capital (OSC) — Domestic Manufacturing Loan Program

Direct loans and loan guarantees (not grants) to finance manufacturing equipment and facilities for critical technologies with both defense and commercial applications.

  • $10M–$150M per loan; ~$4.4B FY2026 loan capacity · Debt
  • Companies investing in equipment/facilities within OSC's ~31 covered technology categories; must show both a defense and a commercial application and the ability to repay.
  • periodic — via Notices of Funding Availability (NOFAs)

How to apply: Watch for an open NOFA, book a pre-application consultation, then submit Application Part 1. Expect finance-grade, slow diligence — this is real project finance.

Official source ↗

Loan

OSC Credit Program — Equipment Finance NOFA

The specific loan track that finances production and test equipment for covered critical-technology manufacturers; OSC's inaugural solicitation drew 200+ applications and ~$8.9B in requests across 38 states.

  • $10M–$150M per loan (first NOFA authorized up to ~$984M) · Debt
  • Manufacturers of covered technologies (advanced manufacturing, microelectronics, edge computing, quantum, solar, mesh networks, and more) with dual-use markets.
  • by NOFA — check for the current open window

How to apply: Read the active Federal Register NOFA, confirm your NAICS/technology fits a covered category, and submit through OSC's application process.

Matching

DPA Title III (Defense Production Act, Title III)

Investment authority (50 U.S.C. §§4531–4534) that scales emerging tech and shores up critical domestic production via grants, purchase commitments, loans, and loan guarantees.

  • project-specific; typically ~$1M–$100M+ · Non-dilutive
  • Companies addressing a documented domestic supply-chain gap the DoD wants closed (materials, components, production capacity).
  • rolling — driven by identified industrial-base shortfalls

How to apply: Most awards flow through the Defense Industrial Base Consortium (DIBC) via Requests for Project Proposals (RPPs) under an OTA — join the consortium to see them.

Matching

IBAS — Industrial Base Analysis and Sustainment Program

A Title 10 authority to identify and fix defense industrial-base risks — funding capability expansion, workforce, and supply-chain resilience for critical items.

  • project-specific (combined DPA + IBAS investment exceeds ~$6.7B since 2015) · Non-dilutive
  • Suppliers of items the DoD has flagged as fragile or single-source within the National Security Innovation Base.
  • rolling — shortfall-driven

How to apply: Typically awarded through the DIBC (Requests for Project Proposals) and DoD industrial-base offices; engage OUSD(A&S) Industrial Base Policy.

Official source ↗

Matching

SSBCI — State Small Business Credit Initiative (U.S. Treasury)

A nearly $10B Treasury program that funds STATE-run capital programs — equity/venture co-investment, loan participation, loan guarantees, and collateral support — the quiet first check most founders never hear about.

  • varies by state program; equity co-invests often $100K–$5M+ · Equity
  • Small businesses in the participating state/territory/Tribal jurisdiction; rules and target industries are set locally.
  • rolling — set by each state's program calendar

How to apply: Find your state's SSBCI administrator and the specific program (venture, loan participation, guarantee) that fits — you apply to the state, not to Treasury.

Official source ↗

OTA

Defense Industrial Base Consortium (DIBC)

The Other Transaction consortium through which many DPA Title III and IBAS scale-up projects are competed and awarded — the practical front door to industrial-base money.

  • project-specific (see individual RPPs) · Non-dilutive
  • Consortium members (traditional and non-traditional contractors, universities, non-profits).
  • rolling — Requests for Project Proposals posted regularly

How to apply: Join the consortium, monitor posted RPPs, and respond to the ones matching your capability.

Official source ↗

Contract

SBIR/STTR (the on-ramp before scale-up)

Phase I/II R&D contracts that fund the prototype and demonstration APFIT later buys — the proof-of-capability step that makes you nominatable.

  • Phase I ~$75K–$250K; Phase II ~$0.75M–$2M+ · Non-dilutive
  • U.S. small businesses (<500 employees, majority U.S.-owned).
  • multiple annual solicitations per component

How to apply: Respond to component solicitations on DoD's SBIR/STTR portal; a strong Phase II record is what earns an APFIT sponsor.

The lifecycle

How funding flows — and where simulation fits

01

Prototype & demonstrate (SBIR/STTR, prototype OTAs)

~$75K–$2M+ · 6–24 months

Prove the capability works and that a DoD end user cares — the record that makes you APFIT-nominatable.

Simulation replaces early physical iterations: Ansys Discovery for fast design exploration, Mechanical for first stress/thermal checks before you cut metal.

02

Win an internal sponsor

$0 (relationship stage) · 3–12 months, overlapping the above

Get a program office / component to want your tech badly enough to nominate it for APFIT — the step founders underestimate most.

A credible production-readiness story — simulation-backed qualification and DFM evidence — makes a sponsor confident the buy will actually field.

03

APFIT production buy (crossing the valley)

avg >$30M, up to $50M · 12–24 months of delivery

Non-dilutive procurement of production quantities so the capability fields at scale instead of dying between prototype and program of record.

Production-scale structural, thermal, and vibration qualification in Ansys Mechanical + Fluent so units pass acceptance testing the first time, not the third.

04

Build the factory (OSC loans, DPA Title III, SSBCI)

$10M–$150M (OSC); project-based (DPA/IBAS/SSBCI) · 1–3 years

Finance the plant, tooling, and equipment to sustain rate production and multiple customers.

Before you borrow $50M for a line: simulate the process itself — additive build parameters, factory CFD/thermal, and tolerance stack-ups — to de-risk yield and ramp.

05

Program of record & rate production

recurring procurement · ongoing

Sustained, funded demand — the far side of the valley.

Digital-twin and reliability models (Sherlock, Granta) keep yield and field reliability high as volume and suppliers change.
