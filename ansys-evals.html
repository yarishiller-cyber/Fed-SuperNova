Straight answers

Questions founders ask

Can I apply to APFIT directly?
No. There's no open application. A DoD program office or component must nominate your project. The practical work is winning an internal sponsor who wants your proven capability enough to put it forward during their nomination cycle. See our APFIT explainer.
What's the difference between APFIT and the Office of Strategic Capital?
APFIT is procurement — it buys production units of your already-proven tech (non-dilutive). The Office of Strategic Capital lends — $10M–$150M in debt to finance the factory and equipment. They solve different stages and work well stacked together.
Is any of this money dilutive?
APFIT and DPA Title III/IBAS are non-dilutive. OSC provides loans (debt — non-dilutive to equity but repaid). SSBCI depends on the specific state program: some are equity co-invest (dilutive), others are loans or guarantees. Always read the individual program terms.
Do I need to be incorporated or funded to start?
No. Stealth-mode and pre-incorporation teams can and should map this path early — the timelines (especially OSC loan diligence and building an APFIT sponsor relationship) are long, so understanding the stack now pays off later. This site is informational, not an application.
How does simulation actually help with funding?
Program offices nominate teams they trust to deliver, and lenders underwrite ramps they believe will yield. A simulation-backed production-readiness story — thermal, structural, additive, and reliability evidence — de-risks their decision and yours. It's engineering rigor that doubles as a stronger pitch.
