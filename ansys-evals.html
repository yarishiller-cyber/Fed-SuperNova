See if you qualify for an Ansys eval — three things, one ask.

Ask us one question — "do I qualify for an evaluation?" — and if the answer is yes, you get three things at once: a short-term Ansys evaluation license for the exact tools your problem needs, live engineering support from an application engineer assigned to your eval, and free self-paced learning content so you ramp up on your own schedule. The concept, straight

What an Ansys evaluation actually is

Search "free Ansys software" and you'll find a lot of half-answers. Here's the real map: Ansys Student is free but size-limited and non-commercial. The Ansys Startup Program is discounted, not free, for companies that qualify. And between those sits the option most founders never hear about: the evaluation license.

Built to answer one question

An evaluation exists so you can answer, with evidence, the question every founder eventually faces: can this software do what I need it to do? Not on a tutorial model — on your design. You run the actual workflow: import your geometry, mesh it, apply your loads or excitations, solve, and judge the results. If Ansys isn't the right fit for your problem, an honest evaluation shows that too — that's the point of evaluating before you commit to anything, and you're free to compare any other solution alongside it.

Full software, real workflow

Evaluations run the same solvers the primes and national labs use. Nothing is watered down: the same Fluent that certifies aircraft thermal systems, the same HFSS that signs off phased-array antennas, the same Mechanical that qualifies flight hardware. What's limited is time — evals are short-term by design, which is exactly why the assigned engineer matters: you get to a credible result inside the window instead of burning it on setup mistakes.

Any combination of physics

Real products rarely live in one physics. Evaluations can be scoped across the portfolio so you test the true multiphysics workflow: fluid-structure interaction (Fluent + Mechanical), electrothermal (HFSS or Maxwell + Icepak), EMI/EMC and signal & power integrity (HFSS, SIwave, Q3D), shock and blast (LS-DYNA), mission modeling (STK). If your device couples three physics, evaluate all three — together.

Who grants it, and how

Evaluations are granted by Ansys and its authorized channel partners at their discretion, subject to eligibility and export-compliance screening. There's a short conversation about your project first — what you're building, what you need to prove — so the eval is scoped to succeed. That conversation is exactly what we set up: tell us what you're working on and we'll connect you with a channel partner that can take it from there.

The part founders underuse

An eval isn't just a test drive — it's your first MVP sprint

The output of a well-run evaluation is a set of credible simulation results on your own design. That's not a demo artifact. That's the core of a virtual prototype — the thing you put in front of investors, and the preliminary feasibility evidence that strengthens federal proposals to DoD, DOE, NASA, NSF and the other SBIR/STTR agencies.

For investors

A stress contour, a flow field, an antenna pattern on your actual design says "we've de-risked the physics" louder than any slide of claims. It shows technical depth and capital efficiency in the same breath.

For federal funding

Grant and contract reviewers reward feasibility evidence. Simulation results from your evaluation can inform the technical approach you describe in Phase I proposals — before you've spent a dollar on physical prototypes. How simulation strengthens SBIR proposals →

For your own roadmap

The fastest way to find out your design needs a bigger heat sink or a stiffer frame is a solver, not a machine shop. Founders routinely leave an eval with a better design than they entered with.

Straight answers

Evaluation questions, answered honestly

Is an Ansys evaluation actually free?
Evaluations are typically provided at no cost to qualified prospects. But we won't pretend anything is automatic: they're granted at the sole discretion of Ansys and its authorized channel partners, subject to eligibility and export-compliance screening. Nothing on this site guarantees one — the honest version is "ask, and you'll get a straight answer fast." More on how we talk about this →
How is an eval different from Ansys Student or a downloadable trial?
Ansys Student is free but capped in model size and licensed for non-commercial, educational use only — you can't build your company's product on it. Public trials (like Discovery's) are great first tastes but limited in scope. An evaluation is the full commercial-grade software, scoped to your project, with an engineer supporting it. Different tool for a different job. The complete "free Ansys" map →
What happens when the evaluation ends?
You decide with real information. Many early-stage companies move to the Ansys Startup Program (deeply discounted bundles for eligible startups — privately held, under 10 years old, under $5M revenue). Others buy commercial licenses, often after funding lands — SBIR awards can pay for the software and simulation work a project needs, per each agency's cost rules. And if Ansys wasn't the right fit, you walk away having spent nothing.
I haven't incorporated yet. Can I still ask?
Yes. Tell us where you are — idea stage, stealth, student team spinning out — and we'll tell you what's realistic today and what unlocks when you incorporate. There are paths at every stage, from free student software to evaluations to the Startup Program. What you can do pre-incorporation →
Will you help us decide if Ansys is even the right choice?
That's what an evaluation is for. Run your problem, compare against anything else you're considering, and decide on evidence. We'd rather you evaluate honestly than buy blind — a founder who proves the tool fits keeps using it for years.
