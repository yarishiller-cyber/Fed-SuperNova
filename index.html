One thing worth knowing

Fed Supernova is the stage — the funders bring the checks.

Fed Supernova is Capital Factory's annual defense-innovation conference in Austin. It doesn't write checks itself — it's where AFWERX, SpaceWERX, DIU, DARPA, the Army, Navy, SBA and others show up to announce and explain their programs. Our site maps every one of those funding sources so you can see the money clearly — then gives you the Ansys firepower to be ready for it. We supply software and engineering, not application advice.
