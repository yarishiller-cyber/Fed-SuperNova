Funding, mapped

A few of the programs

Grant

ARPA-E OPEN Solicitation

ARPA-E's flagship, technology-agnostic call — any transformative energy idea, not tied to a focused program area. Runs roughly every three years.

$250K–$10M (~$3M typical; up to 36 months) · Non-dilutive

Grant

ARPA-E Focused Programs

Targeted, director-driven programs on a specific technical challenge (e.g. fusion materials, long-duration storage, industrial heat). A handful launch each year.

$250K–$10M per award (program budgets ~$20M–$50M) · Non-dilutive

Grant

ARPA-E SCALEUP

Seeding Critical Advances for Leading Energy technologies with Untapped Potential — helps prior ARPA-E awardees bridge the gap to commercial scale.

$5M–$20M (2025 round: 10 awardees, $70M+) · Non-dilutive

What you'll simulate

Ansys applications for this ecosystem

01 · THERMAL RUNAWAY

Battery pack thermal runaway & propagation

Model a single cell venting and going into runaway, then whether it cascades to neighbors. Size vents, spacing, and firewalls so one bad cell doesn't take the whole module. This is the safety case reviewers and certifiers want to see.

FluentMechanical
02 · THERMAL

Pack-level thermal management & cooling loops

Simulate coolant flow, cold plates, and cell-to-cell temperature spread under fast charge and heavy discharge. Keep every cell inside its happy band so cycle life and safety hold up across a real duty cycle.

FluentIcepak
03 · POWER ELECTRONICS

Electrothermal design of inverters & converters

SiC and GaN switches run hot and fast. Co-simulate the switching losses, the heat spreading through the module, and the parasitics that cause voltage overshoot — so the power stage survives its own di/dt.

IcepakSIwaveMaxwell
