The ARPA-E concept paper, step by step

Updated July 29, 2026 · Free educational guide · verify details at the official sources below

Every ARPA-E application starts with a mandatory concept paper — about four pages — submitted through the ARPA-E eXCHANGE portal. Reviewers respond with 'encourage' or 'discourage.' If encouraged, you submit a full application. The concept paper's job is to prove your idea is transformative and technically credible, fast.

Why the concept paper matters so much

ARPA-E won't read a full proposal from anyone. You must first submit a concept paper — typically four pages — and only if reviewers encourage you do you get to submit a full application. Most ideas are filtered here. Treat these four pages as the whole game, not a warm-up.

What goes in four pages

You have room for exactly one thing done well: a transformative idea, quantified. Reviewers look for the impact metric (what changes if this works, in numbers), the technical approach (the physics, and why it's new), and why now (what makes it possible today that wasn't before). Skip the fluff. Every sentence should either state a number or defend one.

The encourage/discourage gate

After the concept paper deadline, ARPA-E returns a short verdict. An 'encourage' means they want to invest reviewer time in your full application — it's a real signal, not a courtesy. A 'discourage' isn't a ban; you can still apply, but you're swimming upstream. Read the feedback carefully; it tells you what they doubt.

If your project needs simulation

A single quantified simulation result inside your four pages does more than a paragraph of adjectives — we can set you up with an Ansys eval and an engineer to produce it.

How to make reviewers believe the number

The single most common weakness is an impact metric that reviewers can't verify. "We'll hit 90% round-trip efficiency" lands very differently when it's backed by a thermal model or a CFD result than when it's a hope. This is why teams put a simulation figure right in the concept paper. It signals that you understand the physics well enough to have already modeled it.

Logistics

Everything runs through the ARPA-E eXCHANGE portal. Register early — account setup and required registrations (SAM.gov and similar) take longer than you expect. Watch the specific FOA for page limits and the exact concept-paper deadline, which is firm. If your idea fits no current focused program, wait for the next OPEN call.

Official sources: ARPA-E eXCHANGE portal · ARPA-E OPEN programs. Figures change; confirm on the official page before relying on them.
