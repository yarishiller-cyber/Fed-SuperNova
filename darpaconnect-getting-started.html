DARPAConnect: the free on-ramp to DARPA

Updated July 29, 2026 · Free educational guide · verify details at the official sources below

DARPAConnect (darpaconnect.us) is DARPA's free onboarding and navigation platform, run in partnership with the Applied Research Institute. It offers webinars, AMAs, office hours, 'meet the PM' sessions, and regional pop-up events to help newcomers — especially small businesses and universities — engage the agency.

Why DARPAConnect exists

DARPA is famously hard to navigate from the outside: six offices, dozens of programs, rotating program managers, and a proposal culture that assumes you already know the rules. DARPAConnect was created to lower that barrier — a free platform, built with the Applied Research Institute, aimed squarely at small businesses and academic teams new to the national-security space.

What's actually on it

DARPAConnect runs a steady calendar of virtual events: webinars on how the enterprise works, Ask-Me-Anything sessions, office hours, one-on-one meetings, and 'meet the PM' style sessions where program managers explain how they think. It also hosts regional pop-up events around the country — recent editions have included regional Mission Connect events and city pop-ups — so you can engage in person.

How to use it well

Register on the platform and treat the early content as your orientation: learn the vocabulary (BAA, abstract, proposers day, OT), figure out which office owns your problem, and watch for events tied to the programs you care about. The AMAs and office hours are your chance to ask real questions without the pressure of a live proposal.

If your project needs simulation

Once you know which program manager to approach, having preliminary simulation evidence in hand makes that first conversation far more productive; an Ansys eval through a partner helps you prepare.

It's the start, not the whole journey

DARPAConnect gets you oriented and connected — it does not, by itself, fund you. The funding still flows through BAAs, seedlings, and SBIR/STTR. Use DARPAConnect to figure out where you fit, then bring a credible, evidence-backed idea to the right program manager.

Next steps

Check the DARPAConnect events calendar and the main DARPA events page for what's coming up, and read what DARPA is if you're starting from zero.
