Hypersonics & aerothermal: how Ansys helps

Above Mach 5 the air itself becomes the enemy: shock heating, real-gas chemistry, and surface temperatures that melt ordinary materials. Physical testing is scarce and brutally expensive, so reviewers expect the thermal-structural story to be told in simulation first.

Illustrative simulation render. Actual studies are built to your geometry and requirements.

The Ansys tools that model it

  • Fluent — High-fidelity CFD resolves the shock structure, aerothermal heating, and real-gas effects across the flight envelope so you can predict wall heat flux without a wind tunnel.
  • Mechanical — Couples those aerothermal loads into the airframe and thermal-protection system (TPS) to predict temperatures, thermal stress, and ablation margins.
  • STK — Places the vehicle in a real trajectory and mission context so the heating and RF-blackout analysis reflects the actual flight profile.
