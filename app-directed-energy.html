Directed energy & optics: how Ansys helps

High-energy laser and electro-optical systems combine precision optics with brutal thermal loads. Optical and multiphysics simulation is how you hold beam quality while the hardware heats up.

Illustrative simulation render. Actual studies are built to your geometry and requirements.

The Ansys tools that model it

  • Zemax OpticStudio — Designs and tolerances the optical train — imaging, beam expanders, and sensors.
  • Speos — Models illumination, stray light, and radiometric performance of electro-optical payloads.
  • HFSS — Handles the RF and antenna side of multi-function directed-energy and sensor apertures.
