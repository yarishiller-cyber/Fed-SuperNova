Simulation Applications

What Defense Innovation Unit (DIU) & NSIC funders want validated.

Reviewers and investors fund technology they believe works. These are the simulation applications that prove it — each paired with the Ansys tools that model it. If your work is on this list, simulation isn't optional; it's your evidence.

Pre-prototype?

Read the MVP playbook first.

How funded hardware teams turn FEA, CFD, and electromagnetics results into the evidence funders score — before machining anything.

The MVP playbook How Ansys evals work
01 · AERODYNAMICS

Fixed-wing and multirotor UAV aero performance

Predict lift, drag, endurance, and prop wash before you cut carbon. CFD resolves rotor-airframe interaction, stall margins, and gust response so your endurance and payload claims survive the pitch. Critical for autonomy and counter-UAS Areas of Interest.

FluentCFX
Funders: DIU autonomy & counter-UAS CSOs, Replicator-2 lane
How Ansys helps →
02 · STRUCTURES

Airframe and interceptor structural integrity

Model stress, modal response, and fatigue on drone airframes, capture nets, and interceptor bodies under launch, maneuver, and impact loads. FEA finds the failure before a $40K test article does, and backs your durability metrics in the OT.

Mechanical
Funders: DIU counter-UAS CSOs, NSIC hardware awards
How Ansys helps →
03 · RF & SENSING

Radar, antenna, and sensor coverage for counter-UAS

Simulate detection range, antenna gain, sidelobes, and multipath for low-cost sensing systems that spot small drones. HFSS designs the aperture; system-level modeling proves the coverage envelope that JIATF-401 challenges demand.

HFSS
Funders: DIU C-sUAS Low-Cost Sensing challenges, Replicator-2
How Ansys helps →
04 · HYDRODYNAMICS

Uncrewed surface & undersea vehicle hydrodynamics

Resolve hull resistance, seakeeping, and propulsion efficiency for autonomous maritime vessels crossing contested littorals. CFD turns a 'how far can it go?' question into a defensible range-and-payload curve for maritime-autonomy solicitations.

Fluent
Funders: DIU maritime autonomy CSOs (USV payload portfolio)
How Ansys helps →
05 · THERMAL

Electronics cooling for dense avionics and payloads

Keep flight computers, radios, and EO/IR payloads inside their thermal limits in a sealed, vibrating enclosure. Icepak models airflow and hot spots so your box doesn't throttle or fail on a hot flightline — a common prototype killer.

Icepak
Funders: DIU autonomy CSOs, NSIC hardware awards
How Ansys helps →
06 · RELIABILITY

Board-level reliability and vibration survival

Predict solder-joint fatigue, thermal cycling, and vibration failure on the PCBs inside your prototype. Sherlock's physics-of-failure analysis turns 'it worked on my bench' into a reliability case a program office will trust for production.

SherlockMechanical
Funders: NSIC dual-use hardware, DIU production OTs
How Ansys helps →
07 · POWER & BATTERIES

Battery thermal runaway and pack design

Model cell-to-cell heat spread, cooling, and runaway propagation for the packs powering drones, USVs, and man-portable systems. Fluent thermal plus structural analysis keeps energy density high without shipping a fire hazard.

FluentMechanical
Funders: DIU energy portfolio, NSIC hardware awards
How Ansys helps →
08 · ELECTRIC PROPULSION

Motor and actuator electromagnetic design

Design the BLDC motors and actuators behind quiet, efficient electric propulsion. Maxwell resolves torque ripple, efficiency maps, and demagnetization risk so your endurance numbers hold across the full duty cycle.

Maxwell
Funders: DIU autonomy & energy CSOs
How Ansys helps →
09 · SIGNAL INTEGRITY

High-speed board and RF front-end integrity

Verify high-speed digital links, power delivery, and EMI on the boards feeding your radios and sensors. SIwave and Q3D catch the signal- and power-integrity problems that make a radio drop packets in the field but pass on the bench.

SIwaveQ3D
Funders: DIU cyber & telecom CSOs, counter-UAS sensing
How Ansys helps →
10 · RF ELECTROTHERMAL

Coupled antenna performance and heat

A high-power transmitter is both an EM and a thermal problem. Co-simulating HFSS with Icepak shows how the antenna radiates while the amplifier heats — so your jammer or radar keeps its pattern under sustained transmit.

HFSSIcepak
Funders: DIU counter-UAS & spectrum CSOs
How Ansys helps →
11 · MISSIONS & ORBITS

Space mission, coverage, and link analysis

Model orbits, revisit times, ground-station contacts, and link budgets for smallsat and space-domain-awareness concepts. STK turns a constellation idea into coverage and latency numbers DIU's space portfolio can evaluate.

STK
Funders: DIU space portfolio, Space Force commercial CSOs
How Ansys helps →
12 · BLAST & IMPACT

Kinetic interceptor and hardening dynamics

Simulate high-speed impact, fragmentation, and blast hardening for kinetic counter-UAS effectors and protected enclosures. LS-DYNA's explicit dynamics captures the millisecond events that physical testing can only observe once.

LS-DYNA
Funders: DIU counter-UAS CSOs, Replicator-2
How Ansys helps →
13 · OPTICS & EO/IR

Camera, laser, and EO/IR payload optics

Design and tolerance the optical trains behind EO/IR sensors, laser designators, and free-space links. Zemax and Speos predict image quality and stray light so your sensor sees the target at the range you claim.

ZemaxSpeos
Funders: DIU sensing & autonomy CSOs, NSIC hardware
How Ansys helps →
14 · MATERIALS

Material selection and lightweighting

Pick and justify composites, alloys, and additively manufactured parts with traceable material data. Granta backs weight-versus-strength trades — essential when every gram of a drone or interceptor costs you range.

GrantaMechanical
Funders: NSIC dual-use hardware, DIU autonomy CSOs
How Ansys helps →
15 · EARLY DESIGN

Rapid concept exploration before you commit

Sweep dozens of geometry and layout options in hours during a 90-day CSO sprint. Discovery gives fast, interactive answers on flow and stress so idea- and stealth-stage teams converge on a buildable concept fast.

Discovery
