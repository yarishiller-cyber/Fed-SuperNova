Simulation Applications

What Army xTech & Army SBIR Funding funders want validated.

Reviewers and investors fund technology they believe works. These are the simulation applications that prove it — each paired with the Ansys tools that model it. If your work is on this list, simulation isn't optional; it's your evidence.

Pre-prototype?

Read the MVP playbook first.

How funded hardware teams turn FEA, CFD, and electromagnetics results into the evidence funders score — before machining anything.

The MVP playbook How Ansys evals work
01 · STRUCTURES

Ground-vehicle hull & chassis strength

Predict stress, fatigue and modal behavior in the hull, frame and mounts of a UGV, tactical truck or trailer before you weld metal. Catch the crack-prone weld toe and the resonance that shakes a payload apart while it's still a CAD model.

MechanicalDiscovery
Funders: xTechSearch, xTech|Edge Strike: Ground, Army SBIR
How Ansys helps →
02 · SURVIVABILITY

Armor & ballistic penetration

Model projectile impact against composite, ceramic and layered armor to see penetration depth, spall and back-face deformation. Iterate on lay-up and thickness in simulation instead of burning through range days and armor coupons.

LS-DYNAAutodyn
Funders: xTechSearch, Army Applied SBIR
How Ansys helps →
03 · BLAST

Underbody blast & mine survivability

Simulate a buried-charge or IED event under a vehicle floor and track hull deformation, seat acceleration and occupant injury metrics. This is the analysis that turns a survivability claim into a defensible number for program reviewers.

LS-DYNAAutodyn
Funders: xTechSearch, xTech|Edge Strike: Ground
How Ansys helps →
04 · MOBILITY

Suspension & terrain durability

Run multibody and structural analysis of suspension, tracks and running gear over rough-terrain load cases. Find the fatigue hotspot in a control arm or track link before a field trial finds it for you.

MechanicalDiscovery
Funders: xTech|Kinetic Reach, Army SBIR
How Ansys helps →
05 · ENERGETICS

Munitions & warhead dynamics

Model detonation, fragmentation and shaped-charge jet formation, plus the airflow and heat around a projectile in flight. Bound the lethality and safety envelope in simulation before any live event.

LS-DYNAAutodynFluent
Funders: Army Applied SBIR, xTechSearch
How Ansys helps →
06 · AERO

Rotorcraft & UAS aerodynamics

Compute lift, drag, rotor download and stability for a small UAS, launched effect or rotorcraft component. Simulation lets you sweep planform and rotor RPM long before wind-tunnel or flight-test money is on the table.

FluentCFX
Funders: xTech|Edge Strike: Ground, xTechPacific
How Ansys helps →
07 · DRIVETRAIN

Rotor drive & gearbox loads

Analyze gear-tooth contact, shaft stress and bearing life in a rotorcraft or UGV drivetrain under combat load spectra. Predict the wear and failure that ground a fleet, from a laptop instead of a teardown.

Mechanical
Funders: Army SBIR, xTech|Kinetic Reach
How Ansys helps →
08 · SOLDIER SYSTEMS

Exoskeletons & wearable load-carriage

Model the structural loads and human-comfort factors of a powered exoskeleton, load frame or helmet mount. Tune stiffness and mass distribution to real dismounted-Soldier movement without a full biomechanics lab.

MechanicalDiscovery
Funders: xTechSearch, Army Applied SBIR
How Ansys helps →
09 · PROTECTION

Body armor & impact composites

Simulate high-rate impact into soft and hard body armor to evaluate energy absorption and back-face signature. Explore new fiber lay-ups and strike-face geometries in software before cutting expensive panels.

LS-DYNAGranta
Funders: xTechSearch, Army SBIR
How Ansys helps →
10 · RF / COUNTER-UAS

Antennas, radar & counter-UAS RF

Design and place antennas, model radar cross-section and predict counter-UAS RF coverage and interference on a real platform. Simulation resolves pattern distortion from the hull and mast that a bench test can't reproduce.

HFSSSBR+
Funders: xTechPacific, Army Applied SBIR
How Ansys helps →
11 · THERMAL

C5ISR electronics cooling

Predict junction temperatures, airflow and hot spots in a ruggedized radio, EW box or compute node sealed inside a vehicle. Get the cooling right in CFD before the electronics throttle or fail in a desert enclosure.

IcepakFluent
Funders: Army SBIR, xTechSearch
How Ansys helps →
12 · RELIABILITY

Electronics vibration & thermal-cycling life

Run physics-of-failure analysis on circuit-card assemblies against MIL-STD vibration, shock and thermal-cycling profiles. Find the solder joint and lead that fatigues out on a tracked vehicle, then fix it in layout.

SherlockMechanical
Funders: Army Applied SBIR, xTech|Inversion
How Ansys helps →
13 · SIGNAL INTEGRITY

High-speed board SI/PI & EMI

Model signal and power integrity on dense sensor and comms boards — impedance, crosstalk, power delivery and EMI. Ansys SIwave and Q3D let you close timing and pass emissions on the schematic, not on the third respin.

SIwaveQ3D
Funders: Army SBIR, xTechSearch
How Ansys helps →
14 · DIRECTED ENERGY

High-energy laser beam & thermal path

Analyze beam propagation, optical mounting stability and the enormous waste-heat load of a directed-energy or high-power RF system. Simulation ties the optics, structures and cooling into one model so the beam stays on target.

ZemaxFluentMechanical
Funders: xTechSearch, Army Applied SBIR
How Ansys helps →
15 · POWER

Batteries & dismounted power thermal safety

Model battery-pack thermal runaway, conformal wearable power and generator cooling for dismounted and vehicle systems. Prove the pack won't cascade into a fire and stays inside its thermal window across a mission profile.

FluentMechanical
Funders: xTech|Kinetic Reach, Army SBIR
How Ansys helps →
16 · EARLY DESIGN

Concept exploration & materials trade studies

At the white-paper stage, sweep dozens of geometry and material options fast to land on a defensible baseline. Ansys Discovery gives near-real-time physics and Granta backs your material choices with real data.

DiscoveryGranta
Funders: All xTech competitions, Army SBIR Phase I
How Ansys helps →
