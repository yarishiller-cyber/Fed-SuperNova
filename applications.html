Simulation Applications

What DoD OTA Consortia — How to Join & Win funders want validated.

Reviewers and investors fund technology they believe works. These are the simulation applications that prove it — each paired with the Ansys tools that model it. If your work is on this list, simulation isn't optional; it's your evidence.

Pre-prototype?

Read the MVP playbook first.

How funded hardware teams turn FEA, CFD, and electromagnetics results into the evidence funders score — before machining anything.

The MVP playbook How Ansys evals work
01 · THERMAL

Cooling a trusted microelectronics package

Junction temperatures and hot spots decide whether a secure ASIC or RF front-end survives its duty cycle. Model die-to-package-to-board heat paths and airflow before you commit to a layout, so your S2MARTS prototype demo doesn't thermally throttle.

IcepakMechanical
Funders: S2MARTS (NSTXL), IWRP
How Ansys helps →
02 · SIGNAL INTEGRITY

SI/PI on a high-speed defense board

Trusted-systems prototypes live or die on clean multi-gigabit links and stable power delivery. Extract impedance, crosstalk and PDN behavior so your microelectronics white paper shows the board actually closes timing.

SIwaveQ3D
Funders: S2MARTS (NSTXL)
How Ansys helps →
03 · ANTENNAS / RF

Antenna and array pattern prediction

Shipboard, airborne and space antennas have to hit gain, sidelobe and coexistence targets in a crowded structure. Simulate radiation patterns and platform interaction to back an IWRP or SpEC RPP with hard EM numbers, not optimism.

HFSS
Funders: IWRP, SpEC, S2MARTS
How Ansys helps →
04 · SPACE STRUCTURES

Satellite thermal-structural survival

A smallsat prototype must survive launch loads and orbital thermal cycling. Run modal, random-vibration and thermal-structural analysis so your SpEC proposal proves the bus holds together from pad to orbit.

MechanicalSTK
Funders: SpEC (NSTXL)
How Ansys helps →
05 · MISSION MODELING

Orbit, coverage and mission analysis

Before you build hardware, prove the concept of operations: revisit times, link budgets, coverage and constellation geometry. A credible mission model is often what makes a space RPP fundable.

STK
Funders: SpEC (NSTXL)
How Ansys helps →
06 · BLAST / IMPACT

Warhead, munition and blast dynamics

Armaments prototypes involve fast, nonlinear physics — fragmentation, penetration, blast loading. Explicit dynamics lets you iterate lethality and survivability virtually before a single range test under a NAC white paper.

LS-DYNAMechanical
Funders: NAC (DOTC)
How Ansys helps →
07 · ENERGETICS / CFD

Propulsion and combustion behavior

Rocket motors, propellants and gun systems are combustion problems. Model reacting flow, pressure and heat transfer to de-risk energetics performance for an armaments or space-launch prototype.

FluentMechanical
Funders: NAC (DOTC), SpEC
How Ansys helps →
08 · MATERIALS / ADDITIVE

Qualifying an industrial-base process

DIBC awards fund domestic manufacturing capacity — castings, forgings, additive, critical materials. Simulate the process and manage material data so your DPA Title III / IBAS white paper shows parts will meet spec at rate.

GrantaMechanical
Funders: DIBC (ATI)
How Ansys helps →
09 · STRUCTURAL FATIGUE

Durability of a re-shored component

Industrial-base parts have to survive service loads and cycles. Run static, fatigue and thermal-structural analysis to prove a domestically-sourced component is a drop-in equal — the crux of many DIBC proposals.

Mechanical
Funders: DIBC (ATI)
How Ansys helps →
10 · MEDICAL DEVICES

Stress and safety of a field medical device

Wearables, closed-loop devices and implants must be safe under real loads and handling. Structural and thermal analysis supports an MTEC prototype and starts the evidence trail regulators will later want.

MechanicalFluent
Funders: MTEC
How Ansys helps →
11 · BIO-FLUIDICS

Aerosol, drug delivery and CBRN transport

Inhaled therapeutics, autoinjectors and countermeasure dispersal are fluid-dynamics problems. CFD predicts droplet size, deposition and plume behavior to strengthen an MTEC or MCDC medical-countermeasure prototype.

Fluent
Funders: MCDC, MTEC
How Ansys helps →
12 · RELIABILITY

Board reliability under shock and vibration

Defense electronics see harsh mechanical and thermal cycling. Physics-of-failure analysis predicts solder-joint and component fatigue so your microelectronics prototype passes environmental testing the first time.

SherlockMechanical
Funders: S2MARTS (NSTXL)
How Ansys helps →
13 · EMI / SPECTRUM

Spectrum coexistence and EMI control

Spectrum-mission systems must share a crowded RF environment without self-jamming. Combined EM and board-level analysis predicts coupling and emissions to satisfy an S2MARTS or IWRP RFS focused on resilient spectrum ops.

HFSSSIwave
Funders: S2MARTS (NSTXL), IWRP
How Ansys helps →
14 · AERO / HYPERSONIC

Aerothermal loads on high-speed vehicles

Launch vehicles, gliders and hypersonic prototypes face brutal aero-heating and pressure. Coupled CFD and structural analysis predicts loads and thermal protection so a space or armaments RPP reads as buildable.

FluentMechanical
Funders: SpEC, NAC
How Ansys helps →
15 · MOTORS / MAGNETICS

Electric actuators and EW power stages

Directed-energy, EW and electric-actuation prototypes hinge on motors, magnetics and power electronics. Low-frequency EM simulation sizes and de-risks these before hardware, supporting IWRP and armaments proposals.

MaxwellIcepak
Funders: IWRP, NAC
How Ansys helps →
16 · OPTICS / SENSORS

ISR optical and photonic payloads

ISR and targeting payloads depend on optical performance across temperature and vibration. Optical and stray-light simulation proves a sensor concept for a space or information-warfare prototype before you cut glass.

ZemaxSpeos
Funders: SpEC, IWRP
How Ansys helps →
