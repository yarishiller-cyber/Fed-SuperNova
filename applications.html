Simulation Applications

The 10 things defense funders want to see validated.

Reviewers fund technology they believe works. These are the ten simulation applications that show up again and again across defense solicitations — each paired with the Ansys tools that model it and the programs that fund it. If your work is on this list, simulation isn't optional. It's your proof.

01 · AEROTHERMAL

Hypersonics & aerothermal

At Mach 5+, flight tests are rare and wind tunnels limited — high-fidelity CFD plus thermal/structural simulation is the enabling technology for airframes, TPS, and RF blackout.

FluentMechanicalSTK
Funders: DARPA · Air Force / SpaceWERX · Army
02 · SURVIVABILITY

Ballistics, blast & impact

Short-duration, severe loading — penetration, fragmentation, and progressive structural failure of armor and vehicles under explicit dynamics.

LS-DYNAAutodynMechanical
Funders: Army xTech · DoD SBIR · DIU
03 · RF / SENSORS

Antenna, RF & radar

Full-wave 3D EM design of antennas and phased arrays, co-site interference, and mission-context RF performance for radar and comms.

HFSSEMITSTK
Funders: Space Force · DARPA · Navy · ME Commons
04 · THERMAL

Electronics thermal management

Chip-, board-, and enclosure-level cooling for SWaP-constrained defense electronics where heat is the limiting factor.

IcepakFluentMechanical
Funders: DoD SBIR · ME Commons · DIU
05 · PROPULSION

Propulsion & combustion

Scramjet combustion, rocket engines, and turbomachinery — reacting-flow CFD coupled with structural and thermal analysis.

FluentCFXMechanical
Funders: NASA · Space Force · DARPA · NSIC
06 · SPACE

Spacecraft & satellites

Structural launch loads, on-orbit thermal cycling, and full mission design — coverage, orbits, and link budgets.

MechanicalFluentSTK / ODTK
Funders: SpaceWERX · NSIC · NASA · DIU
07 · MICROELECTRONICS

Signal & power integrity

PCB, package, and chip-level signal/power integrity and electro-thermal reliability for advanced microelectronics.

SIwaveHFSSRedHawk-SC

Electric propulsion & power electronics

Electric machines, motor drives, and power electronics — electromagnetic design coupled with thermal and flow.

Drone & UAV design

Aerodynamics, lightweight structures, and crash/impact behavior for autonomous air vehicles and Blue UAS platforms.

Beam control, optical systems, and thermal management for high-energy lasers and photonics payloads.
