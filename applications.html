Simulation Applications

What Defense Scale-Up Funding — APFIT & Office of Strategic Capital funders want validated.

Reviewers and investors fund technology they believe works. These are the simulation applications that prove it — each paired with the Ansys tools that model it. If your work is on this list, simulation isn't optional; it's your evidence.

Pre-prototype?

Read the MVP playbook first.

How funded hardware teams turn FEA, CFD, and electromagnetics results into the evidence funders score — before machining anything.

The MVP playbook How Ansys evals work
01 · ADDITIVE

Simulate the additive build before you scale it

Metal and polymer additive is now core to defense scale-up — but a part that prints once in R&D can warp, crack, or miss tolerance at rate. Simulate thermal history, residual stress, and distortion so your first production run yields parts, not scrap.

MechanicalDiscovery
Funders: APFIT production buys, DPA Title III, OSC loans for AM equipment
How Ansys helps →
02 · TOLERANCE

Design-for-manufacturing & tolerance stack-up

Prototypes are hand-fit; production isn't. Model tolerance stack-ups and assembly loads early so parts made across shifts and suppliers still fit and function — the difference between a demo and a fielded product.

MechanicalDiscovery
Funders: APFIT, SBIR Phase II bridging to scale-up
How Ansys helps →
03 · THERMAL

Production-scale thermal qualification

Hardware that ran cool on the bench overheats in a sealed enclosure, a hot bay, or a dense pack. Run conjugate heat-transfer and thermal-structural analysis to qualify designs across the environmental envelope before acceptance testing.

FluentMechanicalIcepak
Funders: APFIT buys requiring MIL-STD qualification
How Ansys helps →
04 · STRUCTURAL

Structural qualification at production volumes

APFIT pays for units that must pass shock, load, and fatigue acceptance. Use FEA to predict stress, modal response, and fatigue life so every unit off the line clears qualification instead of failing after you've built inventory.

Mechanical
Funders: APFIT, DPA Title III
How Ansys helps →
05 · ELECTRONICS

Electronics reliability at scale

Boards that survive one prototype can fail in the field once you build thousands across temperature cycling and vibration. Model solder-joint fatigue and PCB reliability physics-of-failure so field returns don't sink your margins and your reputation.

SherlockIcepak
Funders: APFIT, OSC loans for electronics manufacturing
How Ansys helps →
06 · SEMICONDUCTOR

Microelectronics & advanced packaging integrity

Scaling a chip, module, or advanced package means signal and power integrity have to hold at volume. Extract parasitics and analyze SI/PI and EMI so your microelectronics line yields parts that meet spec — a core OSC covered category.

SIwaveQ3D
Funders: OSC loans (microelectronics), DPA Title III
How Ansys helps →
07 · MATERIALS

Critical-minerals & materials processing

Reshoring critical materials means designing and de-risking real processing equipment — reactors, furnaces, separators. Simulate the flow, mixing, and heat transfer, and manage material data, before you finance the plant.

FluentGranta
Funders: OSC loans, DPA Title III (critical minerals)
How Ansys helps →
08 · FACTORY

Factory & production-line CFD/thermal

A production line has its own physics: airflow, contamination control, heat load, and exhaust. Model the facility before you build it so the environment your process needs actually exists — cheaper to fix in simulation than in concrete.

Fluent
Funders: OSC facility loans, DPA Title III capacity expansion
How Ansys helps →
09 · VIBRATION

Random vibration & shock qualification

Fielded hardware lives on trucks, aircraft, and hulls. Run random-vibration, shock, and drop analysis to qualify enclosures, mounts, and boards to MIL-STD-810 targets before a shaker table finds the weak joint for you.

MechanicalLS-DYNA
Funders: APFIT, SOCOM/Service rapid-fielding buys
How Ansys helps →
10 · RF

Antenna & radar production tuning

PNT, EW, and comms payloads are all over the FY26 APFIT list. Simulate antenna patterns, radomes, and board-to-antenna transitions so RF performance holds unit-to-unit as manufacturing tolerances vary.

HFSSSIwave
Funders: APFIT (PNT, EW, BLOS comms)
How Ansys helps →
11 · BATTERY

Battery pack thermal & abuse safety

Domestic high-performance batteries are an explicit scale-up priority. Model pack thermal behavior, cooling, and thermal-runaway propagation so your energy system passes safety qualification and survives real duty cycles.

FluentMechanicalLS-DYNA
Funders: APFIT (domestic UAS batteries), OSC (battery storage)
How Ansys helps →
12 · AERO

Aerodynamics for drones & munitions at rate

When you're delivering hundreds of airframes or low-cost munitions, small aero and stability changes compound. Use CFD to lock aerodynamic performance and control authority so the production configuration flies like the prototype did.

FluentCFX
Funders: APFIT (attritable UAS, low-cost munitions)
How Ansys helps →
13 · MOTORS

Electric motors & magnetics for propulsion

Electric propulsion and actuation depend on motor efficiency, torque density, and thermal limits. Simulate the electromagnetics and losses so your drivetrain hits performance and thermal targets before tooling the winding line.

MaxwellFluent
Funders: APFIT (autonomous ground/air vehicles), OSC loans
How Ansys helps →
14 · OPTICS

EO/IR & optical payload qualification

Deployable optical and sensor systems have to survive thermal soak and vibration while holding alignment. Model optical performance and thermo-mechanical drift so imaging quality survives the ride to the field and the production tolerances.

ZemaxMechanical
Funders: APFIT (deployable optical systems, Space Force)
How Ansys helps →
15 · ELECTROTHERMAL

RF electrothermal co-design

High-power RF and radar payloads fail where EM meets heat. Couple electromagnetic and thermal analysis so power amplifiers and phased arrays run within limits at production power levels, not just in a cold lab.

HFSSIcepak
Funders: APFIT (EW/radar), OSC loans
How Ansys helps →
16 · YIELD

Manufacturing yield & process robustness

Ramp risk is really yield risk. Use parametric simulation and design-of-experiments to find which process and design variables actually drive scrap, then set robust windows — the analysis a lender or program office loves to see before writing the check.

MechanicalGranta
Funders: OSC loans, DPA Title III, APFIT production quality
How Ansys helps →
