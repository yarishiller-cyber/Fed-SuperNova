Simulation Applications

What Medical Device Funding & FDA-Grade Simulation funders want validated.

Reviewers and investors fund technology they believe works. These are the simulation applications that prove it — each paired with the Ansys tools that model it. If your work is on this list, simulation isn't optional; it's your evidence.

Pre-prototype?

Read the MVP playbook first.

How funded hardware teams turn FEA, CFD, and electromagnetics results into the evidence funders score — before machining anything.

The MVP playbook How Ansys evals work
01 · STRUCTURAL

Stent & implant deployment and fatigue life

Model balloon expansion, recoil, and the millions of cardiac or gait cycles an implant must survive. Predict peak stress, residual strain, and fracture risk before a single sample hits the durability rig. This is the classic FEA claim the FDA expects to see backed by a credibility case.

MechanicalLS-DYNA
Funders: NIH SBIR (NHLBI), MTEC, NSF SBIR
How Ansys helps →
02 · HEMODYNAMICS

Blood pump & LVAD flow and hemolysis

Simulate the pressure-flow curve of an impeller and estimate shear-driven hemolysis and thrombosis risk in blood-contacting hardware. FDA has published benchmark CFD problems for exactly this class of device, so validated flow models carry real regulatory weight.

FluentMechanical
Funders: ARPA-H, NIH SBIR (NHLBI), MTEC
How Ansys helps →
03 · STRUCTURAL-FLUID

Heart valve & TAVI device–anatomy interaction

Couple the fluid and the structure (FSI) to see how a transcatheter valve seats against patient anatomy, how leaflets flex, and where paravalvular leak or calcium interaction shows up. Patient-specific runs feed straight into in-silico trial cohorts.

MechanicalFluent
Funders: NIH SBIR (NHLBI), MTEC
How Ansys helps →
04 · RF SAFETY

MRI RF-induced heating of implants

Predict how an implanted lead, stent, or generator concentrates RF energy and heats surrounding tissue during an MRI scan — the analysis behind an 'MR Conditional' label. Combine the electromagnetic field with the thermal solve to bound worst-case temperature rise.

HFSSMechanical
Funders: NIH SBIR (NIBIB), FDA MDDT pathway
How Ansys helps →
05 · WIRELESS

Wearable & implantable antenna / telemetry

Design antennas that keep a link through skin and tissue, meet SAR limits, and hold up as the body moves. Model detuning near flesh, radiation efficiency, and data-link margin for continuous monitors and implants.

HFSSSIwave
Funders: NIH SBIR (NIBIB), NSF SBIR
How Ansys helps →
06 · DRUG DELIVERY

Inhaler, nebulizer & nasal-spray fluidics

Track droplet breakup, spray plume, and where an aerosol actually deposits in the airway. CFD replaces dozens of cascade-impactor runs and helps you tune device geometry to hit a target particle-size distribution.

Fluent
Funders: NIH SBIR (NHLBI/NIAID), BARDA DRIVe
How Ansys helps →
07 · THERMAL

Ablation: RF, cryo & laser tissue dosing

Predict the lesion — how far heat or cold propagates into tissue and how the boundary evolves — for cardiac, tumor, or nerve ablation. Coupled electro-thermal and bioheat models let you dose energy without over-treating healthy tissue.

MechanicalFluent
Funders: NIH SBIR (NCI/NHLBI), MTEC
How Ansys helps →
08 · RELIABILITY

Implantable electronics reliability & thermal

Find solder-joint fatigue, thermal cycling failures, and vibration weak points in a pulse generator or wearable board before it ships. Physics-of-failure analysis extends device life and supports the reliability claims in your design history file.

SherlockIcepak
Funders: MTEC, NIH SBIR (NINDS)
How Ansys helps →
09 · SIGNAL INTEGRITY

High-speed imaging & sensor front-ends

Keep the signal clean on the dense, fast PCBs behind ultrasound, CT, and optical sensors. Model impedance, crosstalk, and power-delivery noise so a subtle biological signal isn't lost in board-level artifacts.

SIwaveQ3D
Funders: NSF SBIR, NIH SBIR (NIBIB)
How Ansys helps →
10 · ELECTROMAGNETICS

Neurostimulation coils & precision fields

Shape where the field lands — a TMS coil, a deep-brain-stim lead, or a peripheral-nerve cuff — and predict the induced current in target versus off-target tissue. Low-frequency EM modeling turns 'about here' into a quantified dose map.

MaxwellHFSS
Funders: NIH BRAIN SBIR (NINDS), ARPA-H
How Ansys helps →
11 · OPTICS

Endoscope & surgical imaging optical paths

Design the lens stack and illumination for endoscopes, laparoscopes, and laser-delivery systems. Model image quality, stray light, and thermal load in the optical train so the surgeon sees what's actually there.

ZemaxSpeos
Funders: NIH SBIR (NCI/NEI), NSF SBIR
How Ansys helps →
12 · ROBOTICS

Surgical-robot structure, modal & actuators

Analyze arm stiffness, vibration modes, and tip precision under load, plus motor and drive thermal limits. A robot that's stiff and well-damped in simulation is a robot that holds sub-millimeter accuracy in the OR.

MechanicalIcepakMaxwell
Funders: MTEC, ARPA-H, NIH SBIR
How Ansys helps →
13 · RESPIRATORY

Ventilator & respiratory-device flow control

Model valve dynamics, flow paths, and pressure response for ventilators, oxygen concentrators, and CPAP hardware. Predict how the device behaves across patient effort and leak conditions before clinical bench testing.

FluentMechanical
Funders: BARDA DRIVe, NIH SBIR (NHLBI)
How Ansys helps →
14 · MATERIALS

Biocompatible material data & selection

Ground every FEA and fatigue run in real, traceable material properties for nitinol, titanium, PEEK, and medical polymers. A managed materials database keeps your simulation inputs defensible when a reviewer asks where the numbers came from.

GrantaMechanical
Funders: NIH SBIR, MTEC
How Ansys helps →
15 · IN-SILICO TRIAL

Virtual-patient cohorts & credibility evidence

Run your device across a cohort of anatomically varied virtual patients to supplement — sometimes reduce — clinical enrollment. Package the verification, validation, and applicability evidence the FDA guidance and the MDDT program are built to evaluate.

FluentMechanicalHFSS
Funders: FDA MDDT pathway, NIH SBIR, ARPA-H
How Ansys helps →
