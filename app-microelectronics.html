Signal & power integrity: how Ansys helps

At today's data rates, the interconnect is the bottleneck. Signal- and power-integrity simulation catches the timing, noise, and electro-thermal problems that would otherwise surface only in silicon.

Illustrative simulation render. Actual studies are built to your geometry and requirements.

The Ansys tools that model it

  • SIwave — Analyzes signal integrity, power integrity, and EMI on PCBs and IC packages.
  • HFSS — Provides full-wave 3D extraction of critical high-speed channels and connectors.
  • RedHawk-SC — Signs off on-chip power integrity and thermal reliability for advanced nodes and chiplets.
