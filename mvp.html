The MVP Playbook

Your MVP isn't a prototype. It's proof.

In deep tech, the minimum viable product is the minimum credible evidence that your core technical claim is true — and for physics-heavy hardware, that evidence is usually a simulation-built virtual prototype. It's how funded teams de-risk their first DoD OTA Consortia — How to Join & Win proposal and their first raise, before spending on tooling.

The process

Four steps from claim to fundable evidence

1 · Define the one claim

The single technical claim that, if true, makes you fundable. The MVP exists to test that — nothing else.

2 · Model the physics

Build the virtual prototype in the physics that carries the claim: Mechanical (FEA), Fluent (CFD), HFSS/Maxwell (EM), or a coupled model.

3 · Iterate in software

Sweep the design against your requirement. Every failure found in the solver is a prototype you didn't machine.

4 · Package the evidence

Converged results become the performance curves, margin tables, and contour plots funders and investors evaluate.

It works

Teams that funded on simulation evidence

Anduril Industries

Led delivery of the U.S. Army's Next Generation Command & Control (NGC2) prototype, integrating partners like Palantir, Striveworks and Microsoft.

~$99.6M Other Transaction awarded via PEO C3N — a textbook example of an OTA prototype at scale, with a clear path toward production.

C2 integration is software-led, but the fielded edge nodes and radios behind it are exactly the RF, thermal and reliability problems simulation de-risks.

Source ↗

Hero Medical Technologies

Facial Sensing Triage (FST) app — wearable PPG plus camera-based facial analytics to capture vitals for field triage.

Awarded an OTA by MTEC (Dec 2025) to advance the ML-driven emergency-monitoring prototype for military medicine.

Wearable and optical sensing devices lean on structural, thermal and fluidic analysis to prove safety and signal quality.

Source ↗

Neptune Payload team (Lockheed Martin, D-TA, Epiq Design Solutions)

Designed a subminiature multi-mission RF payload for integration across maritime RF missions.

Awarded through the S2MARTS OTA for NSWC Crane — an RF/microelectronics prototype won via the consortium route.

Subminiature RF payloads are HFSS, SIwave and Icepak problems: fit the antenna and electronics in a tiny, hot, EMI-tight volume.

Source ↗

Shield AI

Hivemind autonomy software for uncrewed aircraft; selected as an autonomy provider for the U.S. Air Force Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA) effort.

The Air Force win helped drive a reported ~$12.7B valuation — showing how prototype-stage defense selections compound into scale.

Autonomy rides on airframes whose aero, structures and thermal management are classic CFD and FEA workloads.

Source ↗

BAE Systems FAST Labs (representative of the pathway)

Trusted-microelectronics R&D delivered under the S2MARTS OTA vehicle.

~$22M S2MARTS award for advanced microelectronics — evidence that even a single consortium can move eight- and nine-figure prototype dollars.

Trusted microelectronics work is dense with signal-integrity, thermal and physics-of-failure simulation (SIwave, Icepak, Sherlock).

Source ↗
