The MVP Playbook

Your MVP isn't a prototype. It's proof.

In deep tech, the minimum viable product is the minimum credible evidence that your core technical claim is true — and for physics-heavy hardware, that evidence is usually a simulation-built virtual prototype. It's how funded teams de-risk their first ARPA-E & DOE Energy Innovation Funding proposal and their first raise, before spending on tooling.

The process

Four steps from claim to fundable evidence

1 · Define the one claim

The single technical claim that, if true, makes you fundable. The MVP exists to test that — nothing else.

2 · Model the physics

Build the virtual prototype in the physics that carries the claim: Mechanical (FEA), Fluent (CFD), HFSS/Maxwell (EM), or a coupled model.

3 · Iterate in software

Sweep the design against your requirement. Every failure found in the solver is a prototype you didn't machine.

4 · Package the evidence

Converged results become the performance curves, margin tables, and contour plots funders and investors evaluate.

It works

Teams that funded on simulation evidence

Antora Energy

Thermal batteries that store renewable electricity as heat in carbon blocks and deliver industrial heat and power, using thermophotovoltaics with no moving parts.

Selected by ARPA-E for up to $14.5M (SCALEUP-scale award) to accelerate its combined heat-and-power product, on top of years of earlier ARPA-E support.

High-temperature thermal storage and TPV conversion are exactly the conjugate-heat-transfer and thermal-cycling problems Fluent and Mechanical address.

Source ↗

Fervo Energy

Enhanced geothermal power using horizontal drilling and fracturing techniques borrowed from oil and gas to make carbon-free geothermal viable.

Founded by a Cyclotron Road (LEEP) cohort-2018 fellow; went on to raise well over a billion dollars in follow-on funding.

Downhole tools and wellbore hardware face extreme heat and pressure — the conjugate heat transfer and thermal-mechanical stress simulation described in our geothermal tile.

Source ↗

Sublime Systems

Low-carbon cement made through an electrochemical process instead of a fossil-fired kiln.

Co-founded by an Activate (Cyclotron Road) fellow; scaled from lab bench to a commercial-scale plant with major follow-on and offtake commitments.

Electrochemical reactor scale-up is a coupled fluid-flow, mass-transfer, and thermal problem well suited to Fluent-based modeling.

Source ↗

Form Energy

Multi-day iron-air battery storage designed to hold energy for 100 hours to firm up a renewable grid.

An ARPA-E-supported long-duration storage company that raised substantial follow-on capital and is building U.S. manufacturing.

Long-duration electrochemical systems demand pack-level thermal and safety modeling — the Fluent/Mechanical thermal-runaway and cooling work in our battery tiles.

Source ↗

A representative pre-incorporation team

A solo scientist with a novel electrolyzer concept applies to a LEEP fellowship before forming a company, using the two years to build a prototype and a data package.

Uses stipend + R&D funding and lab access to reach a credible prototype, then converts that evidence into an ARPA-E concept paper or seed round.

Early Discovery and Fluent studies let a single founder show defensible efficiency and thermal numbers long before there's a team to run physical tests.
